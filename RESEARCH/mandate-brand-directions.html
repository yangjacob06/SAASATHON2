Mandate
Brand exploration · 01
A visual starting point for Mandate

Private credit moves
through people.

Mandate gives commercial finance advisers a clearer place to bring deal information together, shape the story, compare recorded lender criteria and keep work moving. The identity should feel capable and considered, while keeping the adviser visibly in control.

What this study is forThree visual territories to react to before polishing a final logo. Each includes a draft mark, type, colour and image language. All are designed to work in the product and in marketing.

A useful first move

Start with clarity. Keep the human in the loop.

Direction 01 is the strongest starting point because it grows from the existing green and lime identity while giving Mandate a more distinctive symbol and a repeatable visual language. Keep the logo simple; let pathways, evidence and human judgement do the storytelling across the brand.
